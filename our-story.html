The proposal

We both knew it was coming - we had talked about it for years before we knew it was the right time. And we decided we wanted to both propose to each other, and make it a bit of a competition. We both love puzzles, and ended up planning treasure hunt proposals for each other - Dhwani set up a treasure hunt all through Manhattan, and Arnesh had created custom versions of all of Dhwani's favorite daily word games. With a bit of a nudge from Arnesh's roommate (thank you, Vinay), Dhwani happened to set the plan in motion on the same day as Arnesh and we ended up proposing to each other at the same time on a boat at Central Park.
